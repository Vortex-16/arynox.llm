PROPOSED SOLUTION
Explanation:                                                                                                         
Course-aware AI tutor that uses RAG to answer questions only from approved lecture materials.    
                                                                                   
Addressing the Problem:                                                                                                                         
Students often rely on AI tools that provide incorrect or irrelevant answers and directly solve homework, 
harming real learning.
Our solution preserves academic integrity by grounding every response in course content and guiding 
students step-by-step using Socratic hints instead of direct solutions.
Innovation: Department-Wise Content Isolation, Confidence-Based Refusal System, Exam Mode Lock, 
Faculty-Controlled AI Scope
